# Personal knowledge management

## Definition

In the founding idea, this is the practice of curating source material and
maintaining a useful, linked personal knowledge base. The human selects sources
and directs emphasis; the LLM maintains summaries, relationships and consistency.
[[sources/llm-wiki-idea-file]]

## Relevance to this project

[[entities/obsidian]] holds the project vault. The wiki compiles a knowledge
layer from sources, while canonical project files control current state.
Optional capture/search tools are candidates rather than required deployments.
