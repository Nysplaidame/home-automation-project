**Type:** knowledge workspace
**Status:** Canonical project vault; optional plugin adoption is unverified.
**Related:** [[concepts/personal-knowledge-management]], [[concepts/rag-vs-wiki-pattern]]

and a separate maintained wiki in `wiki/`. Project documentation remains
authoritative when wiki claims disagree. [[sources/project-readme]]

This records the idea without claiming any plugin is installed or required.
